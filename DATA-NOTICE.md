# Historical observation extract

Derived from Nichols et al. (2024), **Data archive: Longitudinal propagation of aquatic disturbances following the largest wildfire recorded in New Mexico, USA**, version 1.0.1, Zenodo record 12764157. DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.12762324.

Archive license: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. The original archive is `rialgopi/HPCC-wildfire-v1.0.1.zip`; publisher MD5: `42cf095c013aeb3009fa2a773b21f1f9`.

`observations.json` contains derived summaries for three 72-hour historical windows centered on July 9, August 3 and September 10, 2022. Changes include extraction, numeric-entry counting, window/day summaries, unit conversion and an explicitly inferred MDT timezone interpretation. Window-end availability is a simulated replay convention, not a measured telemetry-publication time. These are historical observations.

The extract also contains summaries from the **US Geological Survey**, gauge **08380500**: discharge parameter **00060** in cubic feet per second, plus available point-gauge precipitation parameter **00045** in inches. Station: https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/USGS-08380500/. July rainfall was not retrieved; later packets include available point-gauge observations. The packets preserve source IDs and provenance labels.
